# Woven Earth — 3D

An interactive 3D model of **Woven Earth — the Breathing Screen**: a water-cooled
terracotta spool partition between a rural Punjabi central courtyard (sehan) and an
interior family lounge.

One handmade, hollow **70 mm** clay module is repeated and threaded on cord, with small
**8–12 mm gaps** between modules so water can trickle straight down each strand. Spacing
is tighter around seated eye level and more open above. Frame ≈ **7 ft (2.13 m) tall**.

**How the cooling works (recirculating, active — not rainwater):**
a concealed reservoir and submersible pump sit in the bottom plinth; the pump lifts
water up a hidden riser to a top distribution manifold; it trickles down the strands,
wetting the porous clay so the air crossing the screen is cooled by evaporation; the
water drains to the trough, through a removable filter, and back to the reservoir.

**Controls** — drag to orbit, scroll to zoom (it never spins on its own). Toggle
**Cooling mode** to run the pump and circulate water, or leave it as a dry privacy screen.
