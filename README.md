# Woven Earth — 3D

An interactive 3D model of **Woven Earth**, a terracotta spool courtyard screen for a
rural Punjabi home: one handmade, hollow clay module, repeated and threaded on jute cord
into a breathing partition that works as a screen, an evaporative cooler and a rain-catch.

**Controls**
- **Drag** to orbit, **scroll** to zoom. It never spins on its own.
- **Privacy lever** — pull it (button, or click the lever on the frame) to stagger the
  spools: more privacy, less daylight. Release to align them and let more light in.
- **Water pump** — run it to circulate water through the hollow spools. The motor drives
  water down the columns into the trough at the foot and back up the return pipe to the
  top gutter. The wet clay cools the air crossing the screen.
